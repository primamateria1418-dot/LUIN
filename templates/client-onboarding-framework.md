## Purpose

This framework standardizes how Aria onboards new AI marketing agency clients. It ensures every engagement starts with clarity on scope, expectations, access, timelines, and success metrics — reducing back-and-forth and accelerating time-to-value.

---

## Onboarding Flow (7 Steps)

### Step 1: Proposal & Contract
- Send proposal deck with scope, pricing, and timeline
- Client signs contract + NDA (if applicable)
- Collect payment (first month or deposit)
- **Owner:** Account Director
- **Deliverable:** Signed contract, payment received

### Step 2: Welcome & Intro
- Send welcome email with this onboarding package
- Introduce core team (roles, contacts)
- Share kickoff call scheduling link
- **Owner:** Account Director
- **Deliverable:** Welcome email sent

### Step 3: Kickoff Call
- 60-minute video call with key stakeholders
- Review onboarding package together
- Deep-dive on goals, challenges, and priorities
- Confirm KPIs and success metrics
- Identify any gaps or blockers
- **Owner:** Strategy Lead + Account Director
- **Deliverable:** Kickoff call notes shared within 24 hours

### Step 4: Access & Audit
- Collect all credentials and access (Section 4 of onboarding template)
- Conduct full marketing audit:
  - Current campaigns and performance
  - Tech stack and integrations
  - Content inventory and quality
  - SEO health and rankings
  - Social media presence
  - Email marketing performance
- **Owner:** Execution Lead + Strategy Lead
- **Deliverable:** Audit report + findings deck

### Step 5: Strategy Development
- Build comprehensive marketing strategy:
  - ICP refinement and positioning
  - Channel strategy and prioritization
  - Content strategy and pillars
  - Paid media plan
  - SEO roadmap
  - Email/CRM strategy
  - AI automation opportunities
- Present strategy to client for approval
- **Owner:** Strategy Lead
- **Deliverable:** Strategy document approved

### Step 6: Setup & Launch
- Configure all tech stack and integrations
- Build reporting dashboard
- Create first content calendar (30 days)
- Set up project workspace and communication channels
- Launch first campaigns
- Begin content production
- **Owner:** Execution Lead + Creative
- **Deliverable:** All systems live, first campaigns running

### Step 7: First Monthly Review
- 45-minute review call at end of Month 1
- Present initial results and learnings
- Adjust strategy based on early data
- Plan Month 2 priorities
- **Owner:** Account Director + Strategy Lead
- **Deliverable:** Monthly review deck shared

## Common Pitfalls & Mitigations

| Pitfall | Mitigation |
|---------|------------|
| Client delays providing access | Send access request list immediately after kickoff; set 48-hour deadline |
| Unclear approval workflow | Define and document approval process in kickoff; confirm turnaround SLA |
| Scope creep | Reference signed scope of work at every change request; use change orders |
| Misaligned expectations | Over-communicate in Month 1; send weekly progress updates |
| Missing brand assets | Include asset request in welcome email; schedule dedicated brand deep-dive |
| Client stakeholder misalignment | Identify all stakeholders early; suggest client holds internal alignment meeting |
| Vague success metrics | Force specificity: numbers, baselines, targets, timeframes |
| Tech integration blockers | Audit tech stack before kickoff; identify potential issues early |

## Customization Notes

This framework should be adapted per client:

- **E-commerce clients:** Emphasize Shopify integration, product feed strategy, CRO
- **SaaS clients:** Emphasize content marketing, SEO, product-led growth
- **Local businesses:** Emphasize Google Business, local SEO, review management
- **B2B clients:** Emphasize LinkedIn, content marketing, lead gen
- **High-ticket clients:** Emphasize funnel strategy, CRM automation, retargeting
